Features

Everything between “we should grow” and actually growing

Calico is built for owners who run the business and the front desk. No dashboards to configure, no marketing degree required.

Plan

Set goals you believe in

Goal builder

Suggests a twelve-week target from your own history and from similar businesses nearby. Every assumption is visible and editable.

Baseline report

See how many new customers you really win each month, where they come from, and how many come back for a second visit.

Local benchmarks

Compare your growth and retention with anonymised data from businesses of the same type and size in your region.

Act

A plan sized for a small team

Rapid growth plans

Your goal broken into weekly actions with templates, owners and expected results. Built to run in the gaps between appointments.

Action library

More than 60 proven actions, from review requests to referral offers to lapsed-customer win-backs, each rated by how well it works for businesses like yours.

Ready-made messages

Email and SMS templates written for local businesses. Edit them once, then send from Calico or copy them into the tools you already use.

Track

Know every week whether it’s working

Monday check-ins

One five-minute email each week: progress against the goal, whether you’re on pace, and this week’s actions.

Action results

See which actions brought in new customers and which didn’t, so your next plan starts with what works.

Team view

Share the goal with your staff. Everyone sees the same number and knows which actions are theirs this week.

Connect

Works with the tools you already use

Calico reads customer and visit data from Cliniko, Mindbody, Square, Fresha, Shopify, Timely and Vagaro. Anything else, upload a CSV and we’ll take it from there.
