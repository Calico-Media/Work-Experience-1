Our story

Busy isn’t the same as growing

Our co-founder Tom ran the books for a physio clinic that was fully booked most weeks. It felt like success, but the patient list wasn’t getting any bigger. Every new patient was replacing one who had quietly stopped coming.

The clinic didn’t need more marketing. It needed to know its real numbers, pick one goal it could hit, and do a few specific things every week to get there. Tom built a spreadsheet to do that. Within a year, six other local businesses were using it.

That spreadsheet became Calico. Today 140 businesses use it to set specific, measurable, realistic goals and run a rapid growth plan to reach them. We’re still small, still based in Bristol, and still answer every support email ourselves.

What we believe

Three rules we build by

One number beats ten dashboards

Owners don’t have time for analytics. We show the one number that matters and what to do about it this week.

Realistic beats ambitious

A goal you hit builds momentum. A goal you miss by half teaches your team that goals don’t matter.

Small actions, every week

Growth comes from steady, boring consistency. Our job is to make the boring part easy.
