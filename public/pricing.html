Pricing FAQ

Questions about plans

Is there a free trial?

Instead of a trial we run a free 45-minute goal-setting session. You leave with a real goal and a draft plan, whether or not you sign up.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade from your account settings and we’ll adjust your next invoice.

What counts as a location?

Any site with its own booking calendar or till. Mobile businesses with one calendar count as one location.

Do you offer discounts for charities?

Registered charities and community interest companies get 30% off any plan. Mention it when you book your demo.
